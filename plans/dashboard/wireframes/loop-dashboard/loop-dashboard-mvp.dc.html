Shell — from 3a

The objective name in the top bar is the switcher. Launch auto-resolves to the objective with the most blocking gates; no picker screen. Count row sits above the tabs. "Objective at a glance" is a persistent 300px rail on the Needs-you tab.

One detail surface

Gate rows and board cards open the same 560px drawer. It leads with what is being asked, then the evidence the decision turns on — the revision itself, diffed against the last accepted one — then facts, dependencies and activity. Decision buttons appear only when the target has an open gate.

get_objective_contextFills the switcher, the count row and the whole at-a-glance rail. One call per objective switch, cursor kept for deltas.
list_items · get_itemBoard columns, and the drawer body when the target is a work item. Drawer opens on the cached row and refreshes from get_item.
get_changes(since)Polls the cursor. Updates counts, actor liveness (last tool call per actor) and the queue without reordering the gate the human is reading.
request_approval → decideApprove/Reject writes state against expected_version. 409 refuses the decision instead of overwriting; the drawer reloads and says what changed.
request_attentionQuestion and review gates. Answering records the answer and clears the attention state in one write.
on rejectionThe item does not move. The gate closes, the rationale is written to the target, and the item keeps its column with a "revision requested" blocker line until its actor re-proposes.
thresholdsClaim TTL is shown in the drawer only — cards stay quiet, an expired claim reads as unclaimed. An actor is dormant after 60s without a tool call; that is what triggers a resume prompt.
